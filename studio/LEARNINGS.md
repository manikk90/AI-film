# Studio learnings

The owner's standing rules for the studio agents (social, researcher, writer, director, Mann the editor) and the orchestrator. Every agent reads this file before working. **The newest explicit rule wins.** Entries are added with the `studio-learn` skill.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · kind · rule. Why: … Source: "<owner's words>" (project)`. The kind is `owner` (the owner said it), `result` (real performance numbers) or `observed` (a check we ran).

A separate writer agent writes it, it is critiqued, and the owner approves it before any voice, music, picture or render work. Why: earlier scripts were weak. - 2026-11-08 · observed · Whisper models are blocked here (huggingface.co, openaipublic), but Whisper small as a CTranslate2 int8 model is on npm in 4 parts (`@skillninja/shortmovie-telop-model-p0`…`p3`, MIT; concatenate `model.bin.part0..3`, check `model.sha256`). ## Writer
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Scripts must use hooks, holds, open loops, suspense, cliffhangers or loop endings, everything a video needs to go viral. No plot-summary narration, and never narrate what is already on screen. Why: the Ramayana narration read like a synopsis. Source: "jisko hooks, hold, cliffagnger, suspense sab trike ki jo viral hone k liye jruri sab aaye" (studio)

- 2026-11-08 · owner · Write a real story: a character the viewer follows, a goal, conflict that builds, a turn and an earned ending. A chain of gags, stamps or exhibits is not a story. Why: the naraz-fufa case-file cut (38 s) was rejected at Gate 4; second "script bekar" correction after 2026-11-01. Source: "script bhut bekar kuch to shi ho story atleast 2 mint k video to ho." (naraz-fufa)
- 2026-11-08 · owner · Write the script as one flowing piece of storytelling: full, natural spoken sentences that carry the story from line to line, with real entertainment (comic beats, banter, characters with personality). No 1–2 word lines or stamp-style fragments, except a deliberate punchline. Caveman brevity applies to notes, never to the film's words. Source: "jo script likh rhe ho vo pura ek storytelling flow kre na ki 1 2 words bole. jisme entertainment ho or badiya lge" (naraz-fufa)
