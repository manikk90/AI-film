# Studio learnings

The owner's standing rules for the studio agents (social, researcher, writer, director, Mann the editor) and the orchestrator. Every agent reads this file before working. **The newest explicit rule wins.** Entries are added with the `studio-learn` skill.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · kind · rule. Why: … Source: "<owner's words>" (project)`. The kind is `owner` (the owner said it), `result` (real performance numbers) or `observed` (a check we ran).

## Global
- 2026-11-01 · owner · The director/orchestrator never writes the script. A separate writer agent writes it, it is critiqued, and the owner approves it before any voice, music, picture or render work. Why: earlier scripts were weak. Source: "tumhari script bhut bekar hoti h ... ai writer agent se likhvao ... phle script approval le. fir kaam kre" (studio)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Work as a coordinated team: viral topics → research → script → style, format and duration → making. Agents coordinate through the project files, not in isolation. Source: "ye sab agent ek dusre se cordinate kreke hi kaam kre" (studio)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Record every piece of owner feedback in this file and apply it from then on (the `studio-learn` skill). Source: "jo jo chije batata jau usko update krte jao" (studio)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Talk to the owner in Hinglish. Execute clear tasks without asking permission for routine choices; ask only when the answer changes the result. The script and final-cut gates are the owner's rules, not optional questions. Source: owner preferences (all)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · For a big batch (many styles, many videos), show the list or plan first, then build. Source: "phle list niklao" (style library)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Before making a video, show the owner how it will look: **3 frames at 3 different timecodes** (the hook, the turn or midpoint, the climax or ending), rendered with the real code, plus a music sketch. Full production starts only after the owner approves them (Gate 3). Source: "video banane se phele vo kaisi hogi to uske liye mujhe 3 alag alg time code k frames dikahana" (studio)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Out-of-the-box experiments are allowed. Every format brief and script packet carries one bold option labelled `experiment` beside the safe recommendation. Source: "out of the box experiment allowed h" (studio)

## Editor
- 2026-11-02 · owner · Mann is the AI video editor agent. It edits the owner's raw footage from the owner's script or direction and knows editing, motion graphics, sound design, colour and VFX, using open-source tools (FFmpeg, Whisper, yt-dlp, Hyperframes). Source: "ek ai video editor agent bana jiska naam hoga mann jo video edit krna janta ho, motion graphics, sound designing, colour, vfx sab janta ho ... m tumhe raw data du or script ya direction du tum usko edit kro" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · owner · Before editing the full video, show the storyboard: the numbered frames and what changed since the last version. Render the full edit only after the owner approves it (`approve --words`).
- 2026-11-02 · owner · The owner's job on an edit is only three decisions: finalise the style, lock the script, finalise the storyboard. Mann handles everything else (assets, cut, sound, colour, motion graphics, VFX, render, QC) without asking. Source: "mera kaam sirf style final krna, storyboard ko final krke or script lock krna hoga baki sab tum handle kroge" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · owner · Scope every correction. A correction to the whole video is applied to the whole video. A correction to one part changes only that part: show that part again (its storyboard frames or a video preview of it) for a final check, then render. Approved parts stay untouched. Source: "agar puri video m correction h to puri m kre or agr sirf 1 part m h to sirf usi part ko krke final check krva ke storyboard ya video part. fir render kre" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · owner · When the owner shares footage, the smart engine analyses it first and suggests how to edit it: the best-fitting styles, unique ways it could be edited (one labelled `experiment`), hook moments and footage problems. Source: "jab m tumhe footage du tumhara smart engine usko edit krke ke suggestion de. or kya kya unique way se vo edit ho skti h sab dekhe" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · owner · Keep a library of 50 publicly known editing styles, each understood and written up as a proper style sheet the engine can apply. Work fast, keep assets organised, keep quality high. Source: "publicly available video editing styles nikalo 50 or unko samjho ... ek smart engine banao jaha tum assemble assests manage, fast kaam krna, quality work or unique styles" (ai-editor) Source: "video banane se phle storyboard dikhao kaise frames h or changes h ki nhi fir uske baad process pura kro editing ka" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · owner · Keep a learning part: break down the AI videos and styles the owner shares (cuts, pace, type, motion graphics, colour, sound, VFX) and save a style sheet for each in `editor/styles/`. Source: "ek leaning part jisme m ai videos or styles samjha sku. jisse editing breakdown krke styles samjhe. or style sheet banye" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · owner · Auto-improve: every mistake the owner points out becomes an Editor rule here (and a measurable check in `editor/rules.json` when it can be measured) that Mann applies before every storyboard and checks in every QC. Source: "auto improve krta jaaye jo jo gltiya m usko btata jua unko" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · owner · Mann must know and apply the editing craft rulebook: all types of cuts and continuity, eyelines, shot composition, safe grids and platform safe zones, typography hierarchy, font pairing, colour themes, sound and pacing. Measurable rules are checked automatically on every storyboard and QC (`craft-check`); the rest are a frame-by-frame checklist Mann confirms before showing the owner. Source: "ai editor ko jo rules pta hone chahiye like, all types of cuts, typography heigharchy, font pairingz colour theme, shota composition, eyelines, safe grids, anything sab ko list nikalo or sikho ache se harness krke" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · observed · FFmpeg `drawtext` and the `subtitles` filter mis-shape Devanagari in this build (the pre-base matra ि lands after the consonant, anusvara can drop: "किसी" rendered as "कसी"). Mann renders all burned text through one ASS script with the `ass` filter and `shaping=complex`, and checks Hindi text on the storyboard frames. (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · observed · Face-aware reframing needs OpenCV 4.x (`opencv-python-headless==4.10.0.84`): OpenCV 5 no longer ships the Haar face cascades. Text boxes are measured by rendering the text with the same ASS renderer; character-count estimates over-measured Devanagari titles by about 75 %. (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-07 · owner · Pick the best tool that runs both in the cloud session and on the owner's laptop; when a tool needs a big GPU or a blocked download, say so and use the portable option. Motion graphics therefore use Mann's canvas templates (`motion`), rendered by Playwright Chromium + FFmpeg, not GPU models. Source: "jo best ho or jo cloud m bhi or laptop bhi chl jaaye. saath m ya alag alg vo kro" (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-07 · observed · HyperMotion (pose-guided human animation, vivoCameraResearch) builds on a large video diffusion transformer; Wan2.1-14B-class models need roughly 24 GB+ of GPU memory, and its weights live on Hugging Face (blocked here). It cannot run in this session or on a typical laptop. (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-07 · owner · When an edit needs real footage the owner did not shoot, Mann may pull archive footage, including from movingimagearchive.com, but only with its source, licence and credit recorded (`footage`). No-derivatives licences are refused, non-commercial ones need the owner's say-so, unknown rights are refused, and paid archive clips need the owner's licence reference; attribution goes into the film's CREDITS. Source: "agar edit m real footage ki need hoto yaha se pull ke skta h editor" + https://www.movingimagearchive.com (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-07 · observed · This cloud session's network only reaches package registries (npm, PyPI): movingimagearchive.com, archive.org, commons.wikimedia.org, images-api.nasa.gov and api.pexels.com are blocked. Footage search runs on the laptop or after the owner allows those hosts; in the cloud the owner shares the clip files. (ai-editor)
- 2026-11-02 · observed · In the cloud environment `youtube.com` (yt-dlp), `huggingface.co` and `openaipublic.azureedge.net` (Whisper models) and `cdn.jsdelivr.net` (Hyperframes' GSAP) are blocked; npm and PyPI work. `mg-new`/`mg-render` copy GSAP from npm. - 2026-11-01 · owner · Music gets special attention at every stage: the music brief (hook motif, arc, sync points), a music sketch at the preview gate, recorded samples for lead instruments, every hit and drop checked against the cuts, no copied melodies. Source: "music wale part m specially dhyan rkho sb" (studio)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · BGM, voice, SFX and foley must be top quality. Source: "make sure ki ... bgm or voice sfx foiley vo top quality ka ho" (ramayana)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Synthetic sound effects felt odd (noise-based crowd, synthesised bells, beeping crickets). Prefer recorded samples; avoid synthetic crowd, insects and beeps. Source: "isme sound effect thode odd h" (azaadi-1947)
- 2026-11-01 · observed · A file sent to the owner from a cloud session can be at most 400 MiB. For a long editor package, build it with `--crf 18` (still visually lossless) or send the picture separately. Why: the 2-minute Ramayana package zipped to 433 MiB at CRF 16 and could not be sent; at CRF 18 it was 321 MiB. (ramayana)
- 2026-11-01 · owner · Use ElevenLabs for voice and SFX when it is reachable. The key lives only in the environment variable `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, never in chat or files. Source: "isko use krke sound or voice over banaya kro" (all)
