## Intent and style axes

Choose an approach after the brief. These are working approaches, not an exhaustive official classification; they can overlap.

| Approach | Operational choice | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| Observed naturalism | Plausible construction/use, motivated light | Random grime substituted for realism |
| Designed naturalism | Select credible detail and rhythm | Overdecorated space cannot support action |
| Minimal/reductive | Retain forms needed for the read | Remove necessary story evidence |
| Maximal/dense | Organise abundance with hierarchy | Uniform clutter without a first read |
| Graphic/geometric | Strong shape, grid, silhouette | Flatten required depth/contact |
| Theatrical/tableau | Deliberate formal staging | Impose frontality on intimate action |
| Expressionistic | Permitted space/colour expresses subjectivity | Distort a locked literal scene |
| Surreal/conceptual | Change context, scale or material for an inference | Arbitrary hybrid unrelated to the brief |
| Collaged/assemblaged | Intentional fragment/object relationships | Incoherence in supposedly seamless photography |
| Handmade/process-visible | Specific evidence of craft and making | Accidental AI defects called craft |
| Period/retro | Research period and local adaptation | Mix unrelated decades under a vintage grade |
| Speculative/future | Extrapolate social/use conditions | Generic neon interfaces and glass cities |

Region, period and realism level are separate axes. A naturalistic image can use irony; a minimal image can remain tactile. Analyse a reference's mechanisms rather than importing its entire inventory or a maker's whole signature.

## Place, period and architecture

For a new world establish decision-relevant facts:
1. Region, date, season/weather and occupancy/use.
2. Construction: structure, roof, wall thickness, openings, finish, drainage and repairs.
3. Functional zones: public/private thresholds, routes, work surfaces and camera access.
4. Orientation: adjacent rooms, entrances/exits, landmarks, sources and essential sightlines.
5. Inhabitant relationship: what they maintain, display, repair or hide, where supported.
6. Reference roles: architecture, material, light, composition or identity; do not transfer all fields silently.

Research the actual Indian region rather than a generic "Indian" kit. Pots, palms, scripts, religious marks and craft forms are not interchangeable location tokens. Do not turn class, caste, religion or occupation into an automatic visual stereotype.

**Proposed example:** For a small contemporary contractor's office, research work functions, client seating, drawings/storage and circulation. Those are questions/options for an unlocked design, not permission to add maps, deities, helmets or desks to a locked office shot.

Record the world before reverse angles. Check connecting doors, reciprocal views, stable scale and feasible camera access. Do not move a window for a dramatic frame. Separate the whole master from the actual shot's visible angle.

## Dressing, props and costume

Classify inventory internally as action-essential, character/world evidence or optional atmosphere. Keep optional dressing subordinate to the first read. Ask who owns an object, how it arrives, where it is supported, how it functions and whether it appears in this crop. For a hand transfer preserve hand ownership, contact, orientation and before/after possession. A key must meet its actual lock rather than hover near a door.

Use wear from causes: handling affects contact zones; weather affects exposure; repairs follow construction and available resources. Avoid uniform cinematic scratches. Inspect actual images before claiming correct wear or anatomy.

Keep costume as canonical identity/look assets with scoped states. Palette harmony cannot recolour an approved shirt. Sleeve/jewellery may matter to a hand insert; a full costume biography does not. Preserve facial proportions, hair and skin texture unless those fields are explicitly changed.

## Colour and material

Record significant colour roles: surface/owner, relative value, chroma, context, illumination and permitted progression. Separate material colour, source light and grade. Do not infer emotion from hue alone; source S19 discusses contextual/methodological limits.

Use a palette ratio as an optional aid or an active project's explicit rule. A 60-30-10 split is not a universal quality law. Strict source matching overrides a new palette proposal. Preserve readability without relighting a locked fc image.

| Material | Observe | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| Metal | Reflection, patina, edges, weight/support | Plastic brass; incompatible highlights |
| Wood | Grain, joinery, dents, finish | Grain ignoring construction; floating joints |
| Fabric | Weave, weight, fold tension | Folds ignoring gravity/contact |
| Masonry | Bond, mortar, thickness, exposure | Pattern stretching through openings |
| Glass/water | Reflection, transmission, depth | Arbitrary opacity; inconsistent level |
| Paper/paint | Process, fibre, edge, coverage | Generic vintage texture obscures exact copy |

Material associations depend on use/culture. Oppenheim's fur-covered cup is a sourced art-object case (S24), not permission to change an approved film prop. For an authorised conceptual substitution, explain which functional/meaning relationship changes.

## Graphic/motion systems and change control

For a campaign define invariant idea, shape/grid, palette roles, typography, image treatment, variable content and format adaptations. Check first read at actual viewing size. Test language-dependent wordplay with the intended language. Proof exact copy separately from the picture.

For motion define entry/change/exit, duration/easing, anchors, scale/orientation and focus/light continuity. Motivate transitions. Keep static cameras static. A specification is not proof of a rendered animation.

Pass source/version, intended inference, approved asset bindings, geometry, local light and allowed variants. Location owns connected geometry, Character owns look versions and Director owns shot/sequence decisions under the active workflow. One assistant can do sequential role passes without claiming separate agents ran.

A changed doorway may affect reverse views, bindings and illumination; a text correction need not invalidate geometry. Update actual dependencies and retain provenance. Keep project canon out of this generic reference.
