# AI Art Direction

Translate an intention into a coherent visual world and a readable image or sequence. Work alongside the active Director, Location, Character, Storyboard and Social workflows; preserve their project records and approval gates. Keep reusable methods here and project-specific canon in the current project record.

## Apply the hard rules

1. Apply the latest explicit instruction in its stated scope; preserve remaining approved project decisions and source requirements. Surface a material unresolved contradiction with a concrete recommendation. Resolve routine authorised choices without asking again.
2. Preserve the supplied person's facial identity and photorealistic facial details. Change only requested fields; do not beautify. Keep exact approved character, costume, prop and location-angle tags. Never invent or normalise a binding.
3. Keep world positions, scale, entrances, facing, anatomical hand ownership and prop contact/state stable across angles. Screen-left/right may change with camera reversal; world positions do not move. New approved action authorises only its actual transition.
4. Keep whole-story context internal. Treat every cell/shot prompt as an individual executable unit: include only visible or operationally required cast, objects, local setting, action, focus, camera and light. Retain relevant offscreen positions internally. Include offscreen audio only when source/user requires it; label audio instead of adding its speaker visually.
5. Honour strict references before design defaults. For screenshot-to-video/fm preserve the supplied start frame and allow compatible motion only. For fc preserve existing lighting unless relighting is requested; remove non-scene UI/overlays/watermarks. Richer art direction does not cancel locks.
6. Use authorised source assets for actual edits/generation. A URL, tag or research claim does not prove image inspection. Never claim visual/media QC from a prompt, successful tool call or text-only check.
7. Preserve approved dialogue verbatim. In dialogue prompts put DIALOGUE in the final paragraph, with nothing after it. Omit dialogue text from storyboard stills unless explicitly requested. Keep video prompts short by default; expand when asked.
8. For storyboard prompts use one copy-ready box when requested and frame-local entries. Keep global cast/location lock paragraphs, internal beat maps and explanatory analysis outside executable cells unless asked for them. A whole-world design brief is a different deliverable.
9. Default generated film images to 16:9; follow an explicit ratio. For fx c/composition previews deliver actual preview images above prompts when requested/required by the active workflow. Do not substitute ASCII or claim an ungenerated preview.

## Run the core workflow

### 1. Resolve the task

Identify explanation, research, design brief, executable prompts, actual media or correction. Deliver that artifact. Record internally: beat, intended audience inference/emotion, medium, viewing size/time, source facts, constraints and open choices. Creating a skill does not authorise film production.

### 2. Separate meaning, world, style and execution

Write internally: "The viewer should understand/feel ___ because the visible evidence is ___."
- **Meaning:** contrast, grouping, metaphor, withheld evidence, material association or spatial hierarchy.
- **World:** place, period, inhabitants, use and physical rules.
- **Style:** naturalism/stylisation, graphic language, texture and finish.
- **Execution:** asset/set plan, camera, focus, light, motion and delivery.

Do not infer a concept from look adjectives. Do not equate poverty with dirt, future with neon, luxury with gold or villainy with dark light.

### 3. Resolve decision-relevant unknowns

Inspect supplied references first. Research place/period, craft, material use, signage or feasibility where needed. Browse for explicit research, uncertain niche facts, current capabilities or other facts requiring verification. Prefer primary/institutional/firsthand evidence; record limits. Distinguish creator statements, critic interpretations, heuristics and proposed applications. Do not invent access to a restricted library or claim a complete taxonomy.

### 4. Compare useful directions

For new design compare two or three materially different approaches internally: hierarchy, spatial relation, material logic or meaning. Recommend what serves the beat. Expand exploration when asked; do not impose a quota on every shot. Explore within locks. Change camera/focus rather than moving a locked subject. Reserve conceptual transformations for authorised conceptual work.

### 5. Establish a filmable world

Resolve geometry/access, construction, lived use, prop function/ownership, costume-world relations and motivating light. Keep one authoritative location/master and exact mappings. Follow the Location workflow's existing master-first approval when applicable. Record dependent changes. Omit irrelevant world inventory from a crop's prompt.

### 6. Design attention and sequence

Choose first read, essential secondary evidence and withheld information. Select a camera around fixed blocking. For 2S/OTS compare clean/shared depth, offset profiles, a relevant soft shoulder/head at an edge or deeper staging; use blur only where it serves the beat and preserves action legibility. Derive reactions, inserts and matches from causal/emotional needs. A still selects one instant; video describes a feasible progression.

### 7. Compile the requested output

Use the templates, then strip internal reasoning and unrelated tags. Repeat minimal necessary identity/local view/action/light information for each independent prompt; avoid "same as above." If an essential exact location-angle binding is missing, mark it pending outside executable prompts and request only the missing input; continue unaffected work.

### 8. Review and repair

Check separately: source/locks, depiction, meaning, spatial/material coherence, sequence, reference/tag binding and delivery. Repair the smallest relevant scope; invalidate only affected dependencies. Keep research techniques proposed until inspected task evidence supports adoption. Do not claim new model weights, guaranteed audience effects or unmeasured quality gains.

## Report completion accurately

State what was delivered, inspected and materially pending. Use concise Hinglish by default. An instruction bundle is not an always-running agent, timer, generator or access to every chat.
